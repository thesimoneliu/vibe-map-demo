# Vibe Map demo

A touch-friendly playground for spatial input. Open `index.html` locally or visit https://thesimoneliu.github.io/vibe-map-demo/ on a phone.

Use the menu in the upper-left corner to switch between 23 playable versions:

- **Original:** the reference-inspired chat and Vibe Map. The composer, category strip, and matrix stay at the bottom while the conversation scrolls above them. Moving the point rewrites the unsent user draft; existing chat bubbles do not change.
- **Applications:** music discovery, a drawn DJ journey, decision readiness, fictional decision personas, two-person dating signals, travel trade-offs, outfit mood, writing tone, room lighting, beauty light, hue/saturation/brightness, garment sizing, and gift intuition.
- **Input experiments:** circular mood, reach map, push/pull, a drawn path, painted acceptable region, now-to-wanted points, direct manipulation, dwell to reveal, and bidirectional text/map input.

Tap, drag, draw, paint, or adjust the controls in each version and watch its example output change. The DJ version can play a short original synthesized sound sketch. In the original mode, sending a message adds it to the conversation; simple English and Chinese mood keywords can reposition the point. Matrix points also support arrow keys, with Shift for larger steps.

In Dating signals, move either point to record a short trail, or use the two demo reply buttons to move the fictional participant closer or farther away. The output shows the current distance and direction of change.

All recommendations, people, places, tracks, and personas are illustrative. There are no external data, AI, dating, trading, or account connections. No build step is required.
